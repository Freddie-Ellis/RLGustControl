Tunnel RL system

The actor runs on the tunnel PC and keeps the pitching moment at zero. The learner runs SAC on the laptop GPU. The two machines only exchange files, through a shared folder on the tunnel PC.

Why off-policy

The actor always runs weights a few seconds to minutes old. SAC learns from that data directly and reuses each tunnel sample many times. PPO would have to discard or correct it.

What the critic gets that the actor doesn't

The policy uses only the pressure taps, because that's the sensor it will fly with. The critics also see the balance loads, which they only need during training. The reward is computed from Cm measured by the balance.
